1. What Footfall is

Footfall captures the people you meet at an event. You send a photo of a contact form, a visiting card or an event badge to our WhatsApp number. Footfall reads it, saves it as a lead, and sends the visitor a thank-you message.

Photo on WhatsApp ──► Lead (read and saved) ──► Thank-you message to the visitor ──► Export to Orbit
  • The WhatsApp number is +91 73381 48983.
  • Only one event is live at a time. Every new lead goes into the live event. What you see in the WhatsApp menu, the brochures and the welcome message all belong to that event.
  • Use the menu on the left of the website to move around. The Wiki link opens this page.

2. Who can do what

RoleWhat you can do
Team memberCapture on WhatsApp; see, add and edit leads; add photos; see event photos; run the exports; see who has been sent the thank-you message
AdminEverything a team member can do, plus event control, document types and brochures, products, message wording, the follow-up report, team access, and deleting a lead

Log in at the website with your 10-digit phone number or your email and your password. If a page or button is missing for you, your role does not include it.

3. Capturing leads on WhatsApp

Use the phone number that is registered to you. A number that is not on the team gets the visitor menu instead.

3.1 Send a photo straight away

This is the quickest way, and the one to use at a busy stall.

  1. Send a photo of the contact form with the visiting card pinned to it. One photo per lead. You do not need to say Hi first.
  2. Footfall works out what the photo is (a form, a visiting card or a badge), reads it and saves the lead.
  3. You get a short reply with what it saved.

If your phone has no internet, take the photos anyway and send them later. They go through once the phone is back online.

Send the whole paper in one photo. Two photos sent at the same moment for the same person are handled separately and can end up as two leads.

3.2 Use the menu

Type Hi or menu at any time. You get a list:

  • The document types of the event, such as Contact form, Visiting card, Event badge and Other docs. Pick one, send the photos, tap Done, and answer the short questions. All photos in one go are read together as one lead.
  • Event photos: photos of the event that are saved but not read.
  • Brochures: the team brochures and price lists.
  • Add a note: a voice or text note on a lead.

Type cancel to stop what you are doing. A scan that is left half finished for a while times out and is not saved.

3.3 A photo with the visitor

Send the photo with the caption Visitor Meet. It is saved under Event photos with your caption. Anything else you write as the caption is kept too.

3.4 Voice notes and text notes

  • Pick Add a note in the menu, choose the lead, and send a voice note or text.
  • A voice note sent with no menu choice goes onto the lead you captured in the last 30 minutes.
  • Voice notes are written out as text and saved with the original audio.

3.5 What the reader does

  • It reads the printed and handwritten details: name, organisation, designation, phone, email, address, city, state, pincode, and the answers on the form (demo, workshop, remarks).
  • If a card shows two phone numbers, the first mobile number is the lead's phone and the other goes into the notes.
  • Handwriting that is struck through is left out.
  • On a badge, the visitor category (such as Visitor or Delegate) goes to the notes. The city printed on the badge is the event's city, not the person's.
  • A badge QR code holds only an organiser ID. It does not carry a phone number, so a badge on its own has no number to message.

The reader can be wrong, which is why every lead can be corrected on the website (section 5).

4. What a visitor sees

A visitor who messages the number, for example after scanning the QR code, gets the event's welcome message and this menu:

  • Download brochure: the brochures meant for visitors.
  • Share my requirement: they tell us what they need.
  • Schedule a demo: online or in person.
  • Feedback / voice note: typed, spoken or a photo.

Whatever they send is saved as a lead of the live event. A visitor can type menu at any time to see the list again, and STOP to never be messaged again.

5. The Leads page

  • The list shows the leads of the event you pick at the top, newest first. A whole row opens the lead.
  • Add lead makes a lead by hand. Add photos and Footfall reads them (you see a "Reading the photos…" bar), or type the details yourself.
  • On a lead you can edit every field, rotate and add photos, read the notes, and open the WhatsApp conversations: Team chat (you, when you scanned it) and Visitor chat (the visitor, including replies to the thank-you message).
  • Saving a corrected lead is enough. You do not need to do anything else.

5.1 Flags on a lead

FlagWhat it meansWhat to do
Not scannedThe photos could not be read, or the reader found nothing. The photos are kept.Open the lead and use read again, or type the details and save.
Possible duplicate of #NAnother lead looks like the same person.Check both. If they are the same person, keep one. Then mark it as checked.

5.2 Duplicates and merging

If a card photo arrives after its form, or the same phone number is captured again, Footfall joins them into one lead and only fills in what was blank. It never overwrites what is already there. A name that differs by one misread letter is still treated as the same person when the phone matches.

When one person still ends up as two leads, merge them: on the Leads page press Select to merge, tick the leads (2 to 6, all from the page you are looking at, so search the name first), and press Merge. Pick the lead to keep. It keeps its own details, its blank fields are filled from the others, a value that disagrees goes into its notes, and the photos, notes, messages and chats move to it. The other leads are deleted.

6. The follow-up message

Every lead with a mobile number gets one thank-you message on WhatsApp. It carries an image and four buttons: Download brochure, Share my requirement, Schedule a demo, and Feedback / voice note. Tapping a button works like the visitor menu.

6.1 The rules

  • It goes out by itself, right after a lead is saved, however the lead was captured.
  • It goes out between 9:00 and 21:00 IST. A lead saved at night is sent in the morning.
  • A day's limit of 100 messages applies. Once it is reached, nothing more goes until older ones pass 24 hours.
  • One message per phone number, ever. The same number on another lead, or at another event, is not messaged again.
  • Landlines, team numbers and anyone who replied STOP are never messaged.

6.2 Correcting a number

If the number was read wrongly, fix it on the lead and save. The corrected number is new, so it gets its own message straight away. The wrong number stays marked as messaged.

6.3 When it does not reach

WhatsApp can refuse a message after accepting it. That is outside Footfall's control. The common reasons:

Shown asMeaning
Number is not on WhatsAppThe number has no WhatsApp account, or is wrong. Check the number.
WhatsApp declined itWhatsApp limits marketing messages to a person. Nothing can be done.
On the wayHanded to WhatsApp, no receipt yet. The phone may be off. It can still arrive.

The Follow-up page shows who is waiting, who was skipped and why, and what has been sent.

7. Export

  • Orbit import: a CSV or Excel file in the format Orbit's organisation import takes. Tick the leads to include. Organisations that cannot be imported are flagged with the reason.
  • Complete export: one Excel file with every field of every lead, plus photos and voice notes (as links that open without a login), notes, the WhatsApp chats and the follow-ups. The photo links last 90 days.

9. Questions and answers

I sent a photo and got no reply.

Wait a minute: reading takes about half a minute. If nothing comes, send it again. The photo is not lost if the reader is down: the lead is kept as Not scanned and can be read again from its page.

The reader got the details wrong.

Open the lead on the website, correct it and save.

One person appears as two leads.

This usually means two photos were sent at the same moment. Open both and check, then merge them (section 5.2). Send the whole paper as one photo next time, or use the menu so photos stay together.

A lead did not get the thank-you message.

The common reasons: no phone number, a number that is too short or partly unreadable, a landline, the same number was already messaged, or it is outside 9:00 to 21:00 IST. Fix the number and save, and it is sent.

A visitor says the buttons do nothing.

Ask them to type menu. The list comes back and they can pick from it.

A badge lead has no phone number.

Badges carry an organiser ID, not a phone number. Ask the visitor for a card or a form, or type the number into the lead.

10. Known limitations

  • Photos sent at the same moment are processed separately and can create two leads for one person.
  • A badge on its own has no phone number, so no thank-you message can go to it.
  • WhatsApp can refuse a message to a person after accepting it. The report shows this, but it cannot be fixed from Footfall.
  • Each phone number is messaged once. A second message to the same number needs a decision from an admin.
